## Notes for the teacher

Guests are here to see what the system feels like, not to complete a curriculum. So:

- Probe briefly, then teach something real within a few minutes. A guest who only gets questions will conclude this is a quiz app, which is the exact wrong impression.
- Pick one small idea that can reach a click inside ten minutes, and go all the way to the click rather than covering more ground shallowly.
- Still find their floor, still motivate every step, still make them produce at the end. The demo *is* the method; a watered-down version demonstrates nothing.
